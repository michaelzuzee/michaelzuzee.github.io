A curated collection of learning materials, data sources and research tools for economists, students and policy researchers.

## Econometrics and causal inference

- [Causal Inference: The Mixtape](https://mixtape.scunning.com) — Scott Cunningham's open-access textbook.
- [The Effect](https://theeffectbook.net) — Nick Huntington-Klein's introduction to research design and causality.
- Difference-in-differences resources by [Asjad Naqvi](https://asjadnaqvi.github.io/DiD/), [Jonathan Roth](https://www.jonathandroth.com/did-resources/) and [Pedro Sant'Anna](https://psantanna.com/did-resources/).

## Impact evaluation and randomized controlled trials

- [J-PAL Research Resources](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/research-resources?view=toc) — practical guidance on designing and conducting randomized evaluations.
- [J-PAL: Introduction to Randomized Evaluations](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/introduction-randomized-evaluations) — principles of experimental evaluation.
- [Impact Evaluation in Practice](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sief-trust-fund/publication/impact-evaluation-in-practice) — World Bank and IDB handbook.
- [DIME Analytics Resource Directory](https://worldbank.github.io/dime-data-handbook/resources.html) — tools for empirical research and fieldwork.

## Stata programming and data analysis

- [The Stata Guide](https://medium.com/the-stata-guide/welcome-to-the-stata-guide-12adf81ec3d) — Asjad Naqvi's guides to programming, automation and visualization.
- [Fernando Rios-Avila](https://friosavila.github.io) — Stata programming, visualization and econometric packages.
- [Stata documentation](https://www.stata.com/manuals/) — official reference manuals.

## Python and data science

- [Python for Data Analysis](https://wesmckinney.com/book/) — Wes McKinney's open-access textbook.
- [Python for Data Analysis notebooks](https://github.com/wesm/pydata-book) — companion code and exercises.
- [pandas documentation](https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/) — data manipulation and analysis.
- [Data Science for Economists](https://github.com/uo-ec607/lectures) — Grant McDermott's lecture materials.
- [Software and research tools](https://grantmcdermott.com/software/) — additional resources from Grant McDermott.

## Economic data and survey microdata

- [DataFirst Open Data Portal](https://www.datafirst.uct.ac.za/dataportal/index.php/catalog/central/?page=1&sort_by=title&sort_order=asc&ps=15) — household, individual and enterprise microdata.
- [EconData](https://www.econdata.co.za/signin) — South African macroeconomic data.
- [World Bank Microdata Library](https://microdata.worldbank.org/) — survey datasets and documentation.
- [World Development Indicators](https://databank.worldbank.org/source/world-development-indicators) — international development indicators.
- [World Bank Enterprise Surveys](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/) — firm-level business environment data.
- [ILOSTAT](https://ilostat.ilo.org/) — labour market statistics.

## Reproducible research and academic publishing

- [Git for Economics Research](https://leap.unibocconi.eu/resources/courses/git-for-research-in-economics) — Giacomo Battiston and Jared Gars.
- [GitHub Copilot and VS Code for research](https://paulgp.substack.com/p/setting-up-github-copilot-and-vscode) — Paul Goldsmith-Pinkham's guide.
- [The State of Development Journals, 2024](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/impactevaluations/the-state-of-development-journals-2024--quality--acceptance-rate) — David McKenzie's overview of development economics journals.

Resources are provided for learning and research reference. Access conditions may vary by provider.
