I undertake applied economic research and policy analysis focused on public finance, labour markets, poverty and social policy in sub-Saharan Africa. My experience includes regional consultancy assignments across the Southern African Development Community (SADC) since 2018.

## Selected research and consultancy assignments

**Analysis of the 2024 National Education Budget, Zimbabwe (2023)**

Analysed allocations across early childhood development, primary and secondary education, and higher and tertiary education, and drafted policy recommendations.

**Parliamentary Oversight of Public Debt Management in Selected SADC Countries (2022)**

African Forum and Network on Debt and Development (AFRODAD). Developed the research questionnaire, reviewed the literature, analysed cross-country data and prepared the final policy brief as part of a multidisciplinary consulting team.

**Regional Early Childhood Development Expenditure Analysis and Budget Tracking (2022)**

Zimbabwe National Early Childhood Development Actors (ZINECDA). Analysed regional expenditure, developed Excel-based budget tracking tools and visualisations, and prepared the analytical report, including detailed analysis for Malawi.

**Analysis of Psychosocial Support Budgets in Seven SADC Countries (2018–2019)**

Regional Psychosocial Support Initiative (REPSSI). Conducted regional budget research, prepared the study report and developed validation workshop materials.

**Development of a Budget Analysis Toolkit for Psychosocial Support (2018)**

REPSSI. Designed the budget analysis methodology and technical illustrations and contributed to toolkit development.

## Professional experience

- **Economist**, Southern African Parliamentary Support Trust (2021–2023): national budget analysis, parliamentary briefings, gender-responsive budgeting, disability inclusion, education financing and regional trade policy.
- **Policy Consultant**, AFRODAD (2020–2021): fiscal policy and domestic resource mobilisation research.
- **Statistician**, Zimbabwe National Statistics Agency (2019–2020): national survey data processing and quality control.
- **Research Assistant**, University of Zimbabwe (2017–2018): data collection, analysis and literature reviews.

## Technical expertise

Applied microeconometrics; survey-weighted analysis; household, labour force and enterprise microdata; public expenditure analysis; budget tracking; policy briefs and data visualisation.
