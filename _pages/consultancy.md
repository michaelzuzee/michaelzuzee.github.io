I undertake economic research and policy analysis on public finance, labour markets, poverty and social protection. Since 2018, I have contributed to regional consultancy assignments across SADC, focusing on public expenditure, education financing and public debt management.

## Selected consultancy assignments

- **2023:** Analysis of the 2024 National Education Budget, Zimbabwe.
- **2022:** Parliamentary Oversight of Public Debt Management in Selected SADC Countries (AFRODAD).
- **2022:** Regional Early Childhood Development Expenditure Analysis and Budget Tracking (ZINECDA).
- **2018–2019:** Analysis of Psychosocial Support Budgets in Seven SADC Countries (REPSSI).
- **2018:** Development of a Budget Analysis Toolkit for Psychosocial Support (REPSSI).
